About Us

Built on discipline, not hype

HK FX Ventures is a founder-led forex mentorship house. We exist to replace guesswork with process — giving a small, committed group of traders the structure that institutions have always used, and retail traders rarely get taught.

Who We Are

A tight-knit mentorship group led directly by Haider Khan, built around real accountability rather than open-ended courses that end when the payment clears.

Our Mission

To turn undisciplined trading into a repeatable process — through structured education, strict risk controls, and consistent journaling.

Our Vision

A community of 50 traders who treat the markets like a profession: patient, data-driven, and unemotional about individual trades.

Core Values

Discipline over excitement. Risk management before profit targets. Honesty about losses. No signals, no guarantees — only process.
